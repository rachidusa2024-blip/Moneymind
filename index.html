How it works

Five steps.
You stay in control.

Mina does not make decisions for you. She helps you understand your situation, organize your options, and prepare the right communications — then you decide what to do.

1
Tell Mina your situation
A few simple questions about what brought you here — collections, medical bills, IRS notice, or just needing a plan. No financial history required to start.
2
Upload a document if you have one
Mina reads collection letters, medical bills, IRS notices, and court documents. She explains what they mean in plain language and identifies anything that may be worth reviewing more carefully.
3
Mina explains your options
Based on your state and situation, Mina outlines what protections may apply, what common next steps look like, and what questions are worth asking — including when professional legal help may be appropriate.
4
Mina prepares communications for you
Debt validation letters, cease and desist requests, medical bill disputes, IRS responses — Mina drafts them based on established consumer protection law, ready for you to review and send.
5
You decide every step of the way
Mina provides educational guidance. Nothing happens automatically. You review everything before it is sent. Every decision stays with you. Mina helps you understand — you choose what to do.

Transparency

What Mina
does not do.

We believe trust requires honesty about limitations. Here is what Mina is not, and what it cannot do.

Does not replace attorneysMina provides educational information. For active litigation, complex legal situations, or bankruptcy, a licensed attorney is recommended.
Does not guarantee outcomesMina explains what the law says and what options exist. It cannot predict what a collector, court, or agency will do in your specific case.
Does not act automaticallyNothing happens without your review and decision. Mina prepares guidance and drafts — you decide what to send and when.
Does not contact collectorsMina drafts communications for you to review and send. It does not contact third parties on your behalf.
Does not give tax adviceFor IRS and tax situations, Mina explains options and helps you prepare questions. A qualified tax professional should handle complex matters.
Does not pressure decisionsMina never pushes you toward a specific choice. It explains and organizes — the decision is always yours.
